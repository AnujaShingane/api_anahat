# Emotional Issues & Co-relation of Raga -- ANAHAT Practitioner Tradition

**SAFETY WARNING:** This list associates ragas with serious medical and
psychiatric conditions (dementia, schizophrenia, personality disorders,
Parkinson's disease). Per `config/governance_rules.json` Rule 9 (no medical
advice, no hallucination) and Rule 10 (chakra/raga output is AI-assisted
interpretation, never diagnosis), **this document must never be used to imply
that listening to a raga treats, manages, or diagnoses any of these
conditions.** It is retained for traceability only. `can_influence_raga_
recommendation` is deliberately set to `false` until a clinical lead reviews
and scopes which (if any) entries are appropriate to surface, and in what
framing (e.g. general relaxation/wellbeing support alongside — never instead
of — clinical care).

Source: ANAHAT Music Therapy Training course material, English Batch,
marked "own intellectual property of Anahat."

## Associations (verbatim from source, condition flags added by this KB)

| Personality disorders / dual personality | Vibhas, Komal Rishabh Asawari, Devgandhar | serious psychiatric condition |
| Attention deficit disorders | Kamod, Marubihag, Kirwani | — |

| Boosting confidence | Gauri, Des | — |

devotional practices, not raga selections, and are out of scope for the raga
recommendation engine; noted here only for completeness.
