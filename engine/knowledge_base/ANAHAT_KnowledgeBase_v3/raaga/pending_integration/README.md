# High-Value Source Flagged for Future Integration (Not Yet Extracted)

## Rāgs Around the Clock (Clarke, D. & Rajput, V., 2024)
- Full citation: David Clarke, Music by Vijay Rajput. *Rāgs Around the Clock: A
  Handbook for North Indian Classical Music, with Online Recordings in the
  Khayāl Style*. Cambridge, UK: Open Book Publishers, 2024.
  https://doi.org/10.11647/OBP.0313
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike (CC BY-NC-SA
  4.0) -- explicitly open access, properly attributed in the source file.
- 264 pages, directly relevant to `raaga/structured/raga_samay_chakra_time_wheel.json`
  and `raga_metadata.json` (aroha/avaroha/mood gaps).
- **Not yet extracted** in this pass -- 264 pages of genuine musicological
  scholarship deserves a focused extraction pass rather than a rushed read at
  the tail end of this one. Flagged as the top-priority source for the next
  raga-metadata gap-closing pass, since it's both authoritative AND properly
  licensed (unlike most of this project's other book-length sources).
- License note: "NonCommercial" -- confirm ANAHAT's commercial status before
  any production/commercial use; fine for non-commercial research and
  development in the meantime.

## Explicitly excluded this pass
- *The Oxford History of Western Music, Vol. 1* (Taruskin, Oxford University
  Press, 1017pp) -- commercially copyrighted, no open license, and covers
  Western (not Indian) music history pre-1600 -- not relevant to this
  platform's raga-therapy focus. Excluded, not held.
- *You Can Heal Your Life* (Louise L. Hay) -- commercially copyrighted
  self-help book built around the claim that specific thoughts/beliefs cause
  specific physical diseases. This is the same genre of claim already flagged
  as a safety concern elsewhere in this knowledge base (see
  `Chakra and common-ailments` source material's disease-causation framing) --
  excluded to avoid reinforcing that pattern, not just for licensing reasons.
