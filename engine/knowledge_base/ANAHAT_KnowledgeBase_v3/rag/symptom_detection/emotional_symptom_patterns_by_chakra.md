# Emotional Symptom Patterns by Chakra (Deficiency / Excess)

AUDIT NOTE: added during the cross-check audit -- omitted from the first delivery.
Complements (does not replace) `structured/emotion_to_chakra.json`, which maps named
emotions to chakras; this file maps DEFICIENCY vs EXCESS symptom clusters per chakra,
with a detection-question script for each.

## Root Chakra — Fear and Anxiety Patterns
Deficiency: generalized anxiety disorder, panic attacks, phobias, constant worry, fear of
abandonment, insecurity, feeling unsafe.
Excess: greed/hoarding, materialism, stubbornness, resistance to change, controlling
behavior.
Detection questions: "What makes you feel anxious or afraid?" / "How often do you worry
about basic needs?" / "Do you feel safe in your environment?"

## Sacral Chakra — Emotional Patterns
Deficiency: emotional numbness, depression, inability to feel pleasure, guilt about
enjoyment, shame, sexual repression, emotional rigidity.
Excess: emotional volatility, mood swings, drama addiction, codependency, sexual
addiction, jealousy, obsession.
Detection questions: "How would you describe your emotional range?" / "Do you feel
emotions deeply or stay numb?" / "What's your relationship with pleasure and joy?"

## Solar Plexus Chakra — Power and Control
Deficiency: low self-esteem, victim mentality, powerlessness, shame, self-doubt,
people-pleasing, indecisiveness.
Excess: anger/rage, controlling behavior, perfectionism, workaholism, dominating others,
arrogance, aggression.
Detection questions: "How confident do you feel generally?" / "Where do you feel
powerful/powerless?" / "How do you handle anger?"

## Heart Chakra — Love and Connection
Deficiency: loneliness/isolation, inability to love, bitterness, holding grudges, fear of
intimacy, cold/distant, jealousy.
Excess: codependency, martyrdom, people-pleasing, possessiveness, love addiction, giving
too much, poor boundaries.
Detection questions: "How easy is it to give and receive love?" / "Do you feel connected
or isolated?" / "Can you forgive easily?"

## Throat Chakra — Expression
Deficiency: fear of speaking, shyness, inability to express, dishonesty, secrets, fear of
judgment.
Excess: talking excessively, gossiping, interrupting, lying, verbal aggression, unable to
listen.
Detection questions: "How comfortable are you speaking your truth?" / "Do you hold back
or overshare?" / "How often do you speak versus listen?"

## Third Eye Chakra — Clarity and Intuition
Deficiency: confusion, lack of clarity, poor judgment, disconnected from intuition, narrow
perspective, learning difficulties.
Excess: obsessive thoughts, paranoia, nightmares, hallucinations, living in fantasy,
overthinking.
Detection questions: "How clear is your thinking?" / "Do you trust your intuition?" / "Are
you plagued by racing thoughts?"

## Crown Chakra — Meaning and Purpose
Deficiency: meaninglessness, depression, apathy, spiritual emptiness, cynicism,
materialism only.
Excess: spiritual bypassing, dissociation, spiritual arrogance, ignoring physical needs,
escapism.
Detection questions: "What gives your life meaning?" / "How connected do you feel to
something greater?" / "Do you feel grounded in your spirituality?"
