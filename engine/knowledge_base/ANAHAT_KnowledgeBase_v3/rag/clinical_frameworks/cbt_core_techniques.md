# Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) — Core Techniques

## 1. Thought Record Exercise
When experiencing negative emotions, write down: the situation; automatic thoughts; the
emotions felt; evidence for and against the thoughts; a more balanced perspective.

## 2. Behavioral Activation
Depression often leads to withdrawal and inactivity. Combat this by scheduling pleasant
activities, breaking large tasks into smaller steps, tracking mood and activity levels,
and gradually increasing engagement.

## 3. Mindfulness Techniques
Deep breathing exercises, body scan meditation, present-moment awareness, acceptance of
thoughts without judgment.

## 4. Stress Management
Progressive muscle relaxation, time management strategies, setting healthy boundaries,
building support networks.

## 5. Communication Skills
Active listening techniques, assertiveness training, conflict resolution strategies,
expressing needs clearly.
