# Holistic Wellness Strategies

## Physical Wellness
Regular exercise routine, balanced nutrition, adequate sleep (7-9 hours), regular medical
check-ups.

## Mental Wellness
Stress management techniques, mindfulness practices, cognitive flexibility training,
emotional regulation skills.

## Social Wellness
Building meaningful relationships, community involvement, setting healthy boundaries,
communication skills.

## Lifestyle Factors
Work-life balance, time in nature, creative expression, spiritual practices.

## Self-Care Practices
Daily routines that nurture, setting realistic goals, celebrating small wins, practicing
self-compassion.
