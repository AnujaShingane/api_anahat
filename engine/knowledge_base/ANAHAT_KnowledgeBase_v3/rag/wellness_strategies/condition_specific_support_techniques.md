# Condition-Specific Support Technique Reference

(Crisis hotline numbers have been moved to `structured/emergency_contacts.json` for
exact-match hardcoded retrieval -- see that file, not this one, for crisis contacts.)

## Anxiety Management
Grounding techniques (5-4-3-2-1 method), square breathing (4-4-4-4 pattern), progressive
muscle relaxation, cognitive restructuring.

## Depression Support
Behavioral activation, pleasant activity scheduling, sleep hygiene practices, social
connection building.

## Work-Life Balance
Setting boundaries, time management, stress reduction techniques, career counseling
resources.

## Relationship Support
Communication workshops, couples therapy resources, family counseling, support groups.
