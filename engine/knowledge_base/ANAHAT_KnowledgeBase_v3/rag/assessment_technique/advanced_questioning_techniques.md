# Advanced Questioning Techniques

1. **The Empty Chair Technique** — for exploring relationships or internal conflicts by
   addressing an absent person or part of the self as if present.
2. **Parts Work Questioning** — "There's a part of you that... and another part that..."
   surfaces internal conflict without forcing resolution.
3. **Somatic Inquiry** — "Where in your body do you feel that?" grounds emotional content
   in physical sensation.
4. **Timeline Exploration** — connecting past, present, and future to build a coherent
   narrative of change.
5. **Externalizing Questions** — "If your depression could speak, what would it say?"
   separates the person from the problem.
