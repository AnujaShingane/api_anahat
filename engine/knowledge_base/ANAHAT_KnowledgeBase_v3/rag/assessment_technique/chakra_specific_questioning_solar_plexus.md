# Solar Plexus Chakra — Exploration Questions

Themes covered: Personal Power, Boundary, Self-Worth, Will and Determination.

### Personal Power Theme
Opening: "Where in your life do you feel most powerful?", "When do you feel confident and
in control?", "Tell me about making an important decision recently..."
Deepening: "Where did you learn about your own power?", "What situations make you feel
powerless?", "How do you handle conflicts or confrontations?"

### Boundary Theme
"How easy is it for you to say 'no'?", "What happens when someone crosses your
boundaries?", "Describe your relationship with setting limits..."

### Self-Worth Theme
"How would you describe your self-esteem?", "What do you like/appreciate about
yourself?", "When do you feel 'not enough'?"

### Will and Determination Theme
"How do you approach challenges?", "What goals are you actively pursuing?", "When do you
give up versus persevere?"
Exception: "Tell me about a time you felt truly confident...", "When have you
successfully asserted yourself?"
