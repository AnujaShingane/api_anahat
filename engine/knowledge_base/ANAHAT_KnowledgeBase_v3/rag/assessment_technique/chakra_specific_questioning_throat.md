# Throat Chakra — Exploration Questions

Themes covered: Self-Expression, Unexpressed Needs, Assertiveness, Being Heard,
Communication Under Conflict, Authenticity.

Machine-readable version with IDs/follow-ups: `structured/chakra_specific_questions.json`
(CQ-THR-01 through CQ-THR-06).

### Self-Expression & Unexpressed Needs
"How comfortable are you expressing what you really think or feel?", "Is there
anything you often want to say but hold back?", "What usually stops you from
saying it?"

### Assertiveness & Being Heard
"How easy is it for you to ask for what you need?", "Do you generally feel
heard when communicating with others?", "What does it feel like when you
don't feel heard?"

### Communication Under Conflict
"How comfortable are you expressing yourself when there is disagreement?",
"Do you tend to go quiet, or do you speak up, when you disagree with
someone?"

### Authenticity
"How often do you feel you can be your authentic self around others?"

None of these questions name the Throat Chakra or assert a blockage to the
patient -- they investigate self-expression/communication as a lived
experience first, matching the neutrality standard used for Root/Sacral/
Solar Plexus/Heart.
