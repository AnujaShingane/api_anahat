# Heart Chakra — Exploration Questions

Themes covered: Love and Connection, Grief and Loss, Forgiveness, Compassion.

### Love and Connection Theme
Opening: "How easy is it for you to give and receive love?", "Tell me about the
relationships that matter most...", "What does love mean to you?"
Deepening: "When did you first experience unconditional love?", "How open or guarded is
your heart currently?", "What would it mean to love yourself fully?"

### Grief and Loss Theme
"What losses have shaped your heart?", "How do you handle grief when it arises?", "Is
there unresolved grief affecting you now?"

### Forgiveness Theme
"How easy is it for you to forgive?", "Who/what do you need to forgive (including
yourself)?", "What would forgiveness free you from?"

### Compassion Theme
"How compassionate are you toward yourself?", "When do you feel most empathetic toward
others?", "What blocks your compassion?"
Circular: "How would loved ones describe your capacity to love?", "What would your inner
child say about feeling loved?"
