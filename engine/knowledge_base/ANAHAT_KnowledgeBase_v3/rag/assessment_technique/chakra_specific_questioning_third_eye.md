# Third Eye Chakra — Exploration Questions

Themes covered: Concentration, Focus Disruptors, Decision-Making,
Overthinking, Mental Clarity, Coping with Uncertainty.

Machine-readable version with IDs/follow-ups: `structured/chakra_specific_questions.json`
(CQ-AJN-01 through CQ-AJN-06).

### Concentration & Focus
"How easy is it for you to maintain concentration?", "What usually makes it
difficult for you to focus?"

### Decision-Making & Overthinking
"How comfortable are you making important decisions?", "Do you tend to
overthink decisions after making them?"

### Mental Clarity & Uncertainty
"How clear does your thinking usually feel?", "What helps you think clearly
when you feel uncertain?"

**Safety note:** these questions must never be used to diagnose ADHD or any
other clinical attention/cognitive condition (see governance rule 9, and
gap-filling instruction 3F). Persistent, severe concentration/decision-making
difficulty disclosed here should route to the existing clinical-context
workflow, not be resolved through chakra scoring alone.
