# Root Chakra — Exploration Questions

Themes covered: Safety and Security, Financial Stability, Body Connection.

### Safety and Security Theme
Opening: "What makes you feel safe and secure?", "How grounded do you feel in your daily
life?", "Tell me about your living situation and how it feels..."
Deepening: "When did you first remember feeling unsafe?", "What would complete security
look like for you?", "How do you know when you're 'home' somewhere?"
Scaling: "Rate your sense of physical safety right now (1-10)", "How secure do you feel
about meeting basic needs?"
Exception: "When do you feel most grounded?", "What helps you feel more secure?"

### Financial Stability Theme
"How do you feel about your financial situation?", "What money concerns keep you up at
night?", "When do you feel most/least financially secure?"

### Body Connection Theme
"How connected do you feel to your physical body?", "Where in your body do you feel
anxiety/stress?", "Do you feel more 'in your head' or 'in your body'?"
