# Crown Chakra — Exploration Questions

Themes covered: Meaning & Purpose, Life Transitions, Peace & Connection,
Spiritual Beliefs (opt-in only).

Machine-readable version with IDs/follow-ups: `structured/chakra_specific_questions.json`
(CQ-CRN-01 through CQ-CRN-05).

**Handle this chakra with particular sensitivity.** Per gap-filling
instruction 3D: do not assume the patient is religious or spiritual, keep
these questions optional, and let the patient decline.

### Meaning & Purpose
"Do you currently feel that your life has meaning or purpose?", "What gives
you a sense of purpose at this stage of life?"

### Life Transitions
"Have your priorities or sense of purpose changed recently?"

### Peace & Connection
"Are there activities that give you a sense of peace or connection?" — framed
so a secular answer (nature, family, work, hobbies) is just as valid as a
spiritual one.

### Spiritual Beliefs — explicit opt-in gate
"Would you like your personal beliefs or spirituality to be considered as
part of your assessment?" (CQ-CRN-05). If the patient declines, do not raise
spiritual/religious content again in the session.
