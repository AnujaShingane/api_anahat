# Future Plans — Open-Ended Question Bank

- How does your perfect future appear to you?
- Do you have any concerns regarding your future?
- How well do your present objectives fit with your long-term plan?
- Which goals are more important to you, long-term or short-term?
- What impact do your core values have on your future plans?
- How do you respond to unanticipated modifications to your long-term plans?
- How do you strike a balance between your career and personal goals?
- Do any self-limiting thoughts prevent you from pursuing your goals?
- How do you handle uncertainty regarding the future in your life?
- How do you make sure your plans for the future help you feel fulfilled?
- What has brought you to therapy?
- What are the key problems you would like to explore or solve?
- How is your appetite?
- Are you eating more or less than usual?
- How are you sleeping?
- Are you sleeping more or less than usual?
- How is the quality of your sleep?
- How often do you exercise?
- Where do you go for support when you need it?
- Do you have a network of supportive relationships with others?
- Have you experienced any previous struggles with your mental health?
- Have you attended any previous therapy or treatment programs?
- How are your relationships with your family?
- How often are you in touch with them?
- Are you in an intimate relationship with a partner?
- How is that going?
- Are there any aspects of your daily life that you struggle with right now?
- Are there any areas of your work and/or school life that you find challenging?
