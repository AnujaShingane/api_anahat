# Relationships — Open-Ended Question Bank

- In what ways does your present approach to building connections with others draw from your past relationships, both good and bad?
- In your relationships, what are the most important values you look for, and how do they make you feel satisfied overall?
- Do you see any trends in your previous relationships that you would like to alter or enhance?
- What techniques do you employ to protect healthy communication in your relationships, and how do you handle conflict in them?
- How do you handle disagreements in a relationship and what aspects of your expectations match those of your partner?
- How do you strike a good balance and set priorities for your friendships, family ties, and romantic relationships?
- How do you establish and maintain trust with others, and what part does trust play in your relationships?
- What are some changes you would like to make to the way you give and receive love in your relationships?
- Do you have any concerns or insecurities that might be getting in the way of your capacity to build and sustain meaningful relationships?
- In order to build mutual respect and understanding, how do you establish and convey limits in your relationships?
