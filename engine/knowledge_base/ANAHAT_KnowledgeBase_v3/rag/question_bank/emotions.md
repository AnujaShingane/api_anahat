# Emotions — Open-Ended Question Bank

- In three words, how would you sum up your emotional condition right now?
- Which certain situations or incidents make you feel happy?
- How do you manage intense feelings on a daily basis?
- Is there a particular memory from your early years that still affects your feelings now?
- How do your fundamental concepts influence the way you feel and behave?
- Is there a good way for you to let out your anger?
- How do you do that?
- Do you have any feelings that you usually repress?
- If yes, why?
- What effects do your feelings have on the people you interact with?
- Which forms of self-care do you use to keep your emotional health in check?
- Can you identify any stress or anxiety-related emotional triggers in your life?
- How do you tell the difference between real feelings and ones that are influenced by other forces?
- Do you have any particular objectives in mind to strengthen your emotional resilience?
- Regarding the emotional development, how at ease are you with vulnerability?
- How can self-reflection help you recognize and control your emotions?
