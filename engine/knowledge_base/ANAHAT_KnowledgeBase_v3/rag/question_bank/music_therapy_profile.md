# Music Therapy Profile — Open-Ended Question Bank

- What has your relationship with music been like so far -- do you play an instrument, or are you more of a listener?
- What kind of music do you naturally gravitate towards -- classical, devotional, Bollywood, or something else?
- Is there a particular instrument or sound -- like flute, tabla, piano, or vocals -- that you feel especially drawn to?
- When you listen to music to feel better, do you look for something calming, uplifting, spiritual, or something else?
- How often do you find yourself listening to music in a typical week?
- When you do listen, how long do you usually stay with it -- a few minutes, or longer stretches?
- What usually draws you to a song -- the mood it creates, the lyrics, the melody, or the rhythm?
- Is there any kind of sound or music that bothers you or feels overwhelming -- loud sounds, certain songs, or anything like that?
- Do devotional or meditative sounds, like chanting or nature sounds, resonate with you, or do you prefer more universal music?
