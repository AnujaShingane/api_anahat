# Diet — Open-Ended Question Bank

- What kind of diet do you follow -- vegetarian, non-vegetarian, vegan?
- How regular are your meals -- three set meals a day, frequent snacking, or fairly irregular?
- What kind of food do you find yourself reaching for most -- comfort food, healthier options, or fast food?
- How much water would you say you drink through the day?
- Would you describe your eating habits as mindful, more emotional, or a bit fussy?
- Do you have any specific dietary needs or restrictions I should know about -- low sugar, low salt, gluten-free, allergies?
- Are there particular cravings you notice often -- sweets, salty snacks, spicy food?
