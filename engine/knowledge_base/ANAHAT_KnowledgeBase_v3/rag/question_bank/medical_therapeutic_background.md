# Medical & Therapeutic Background — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Do you have any ongoing medical conditions I should know about, such as diabetes, thyroid, heart, or any other health issue?
- Are you currently taking any medication?
- If so, could you tell me what it's for?
- Are you currently undergoing any other kind of therapy or treatment, such as physiotherapy, counselling, Ayurveda, or yoga?
- Have you ever spoken with a counsellor, psychologist, or psychiatrist before, or been part of a support group?
- What are you hoping this music therapy will help you with the most right now?
- On a scale of 0 to 10, how much physical pain or discomfort would you say you're experiencing these days?
- Overall, how would you describe your general health right now -- excellent, good, fair, or poor?
- Do you live with any long-term or chronic illness?
- Do you have any allergies -- to food, medication, or anything in your environment -- that I should be aware of?
