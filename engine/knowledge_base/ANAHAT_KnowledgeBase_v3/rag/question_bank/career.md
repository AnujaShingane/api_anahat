# Career — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Can you spot any trends in the way you decide on a career, and if so, how have they affected your route in the workplace?
- What coping strategies do you employ to deal with the pressure and stress you face at work?
- How does the career you’re pursuing now help you feel fulfilled and purposeful?
- Is there anything that you are afraid of or insecure about that could be impeding your career growth?
- If so, how do you intend to overcome it?
- In making job decisions, how do you strike a balance between following your passion and being realistic?
- How important is work-life balance to your sense of overall job satisfaction?
- How can you make sure your health and well-being are top priorities at work?
- How do your professional decisions fit into your long-term financial objectives?
- What is your definition of success in your career, and has it changed over time?
