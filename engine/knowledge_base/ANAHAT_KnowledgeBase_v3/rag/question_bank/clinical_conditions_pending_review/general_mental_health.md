# General Mental Health Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How would you rate your overall mental health on a scale of 1-10?
- How often do you feel stressed?
- How often do you feel anxious?
- How often do you feel depressed?
- How often do you have trouble sleeping?
- How often do you feel overwhelmed?
- How often do you feel lonely?
- How often do you feel happy?
- How often do you feel motivated?
- How often do you feel tired?
- How often do you have suicidal thoughts?
- Have you been diagnosed with a mental health condition?
- Are you currently receiving treatment for a mental health condition?
- How effective is your current treatment?
