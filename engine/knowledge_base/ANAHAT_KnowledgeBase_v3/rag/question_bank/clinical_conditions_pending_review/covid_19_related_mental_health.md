# COVID-19 Related Mental Health Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How has the pandemic affected your mental health?
- How often do you feel anxious about the pandemic?
- How often do you feel isolated due to lockdowns?
- How often do you have trouble sleeping because of pandemic-related worries?
- How often do you feel stressed about health risks?
- How often do you experience changes in mood due to the pandemic?
- How often do you feel overwhelmed by news about the pandemic?
- How often do you have trouble concentrating due to pandemic concerns?
- How often do you feel grateful or positive despite the challenges?
- How often do you seek support from others during the pandemic?
