# Eating Disorder Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How often do you worry about your weight or body shape?
- How often do you restrict your food intake?
- How often do you binge eat?
- How often do you purge after eating?
- How often do you exercise excessively to control your weight?
- How often do you feel guilty after eating?
- How often do you avoid social situations involving food?
- How often do you have a distorted body image?
- How often do you feel out of control during eating episodes?
- How often do you use laxatives or diuretics to control weight?
- Substance Abuse Questions How often do you use alcohol or drugs?
- How often do you feel like you need to use substances to function?
- How often do you experience withdrawal symptoms when not using?
- How often do you continue using despite negative consequences?
- How often do you spend a lot of time obtaining or using substances?
- How often do you neglect responsibilities due to substance use?
- How often do you have cravings for substances?
- How often do you use substances in dangerous situations?
- How often do you try to cut down or stop but can't?
- How often do you experience blackouts or memory loss from substance use?
