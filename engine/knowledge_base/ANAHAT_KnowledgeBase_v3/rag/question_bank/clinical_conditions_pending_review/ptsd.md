# PTSD Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- Have you ever experienced a traumatic event?
- How often do you have flashbacks or nightmares about the event?
- How often do you avoid reminders of the traumatic event?
- How often do you feel emotionally numb or detached?
- How often do you feel jumpy or easily startled?
- How often do you have trouble sleeping due to the event?
- How often do you feel irritable or angry?
- How often do you have difficulty concentrating?
- How often do you feel guilty or blame yourself for the event?
- How often do you feel like your life has been shortened by the event?
