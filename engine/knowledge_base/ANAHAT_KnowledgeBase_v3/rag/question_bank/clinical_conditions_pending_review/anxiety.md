# Anxiety Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How often do you feel nervous or anxious?
- How often do you have panic attacks?
- How often do you avoid certain situations because of anxiety?
- How often do you have trouble concentrating because of anxiety?
- How often do you feel restless or on edge?
- How often do you worry excessively about everyday things?
- How often do you have physical symptoms like sweating or trembling due to anxiety?
- How often do you feel like something terrible is going to happen?
- How often do you have difficulty controlling your worry?
- How often do you avoid social situations because of anxiety?
