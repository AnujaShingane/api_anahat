# Schizophrenia Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How often do you hear voices or see things that others don't?
- How often do you have disorganized thoughts or speech?
- How often do you feel paranoid or suspicious?
- How often do you have delusions (false beliefs)?
- How often do you feel emotionally flat or numb?
- How often do you have trouble with motivation or daily activities?
- How often do you experience hallucinations?
- How often do you have trouble distinguishing reality from fantasy?
- How often do you feel socially withdrawn?
- How often do you have cognitive difficulties (e.g., memory or attention problems)?
