# Stress Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How often do you feel stressed?
- What are your main sources of stress?
- How do you cope with stress?
- How often do you feel overwhelmed by stress?
- How does stress affect your daily life?
- How often do you feel like you can't handle your responsibilities?
- How often do you experience physical symptoms of stress (e.g., headaches, muscle tension)?
- How often do you feel like you're losing control?
- How often do you have trouble relaxing?
- How often do you feel burned out?
