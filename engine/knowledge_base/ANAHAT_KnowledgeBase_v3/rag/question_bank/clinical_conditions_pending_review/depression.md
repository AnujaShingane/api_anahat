# Depression Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How often do you feel sad or down?
- How often do you lose interest in activities you used to enjoy?
- How often do you feel worthless or guilty?
- How often do you have trouble sleeping?
- How often do you feel tired or have low energy?
- How often do you have thoughts of death or suicide?
- How often do you have trouble concentrating or making decisions?
- How often do you feel hopeless about the future?
- How often do you have changes in your appetite?
- How often do you feel irritable or angry?
