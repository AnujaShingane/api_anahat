# OCD Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How often do you have unwanted, intrusive thoughts?
- How often do you perform repetitive behaviors or rituals?
- How often do you feel compelled to check things repeatedly?
- How often do you feel anxious if you don't perform rituals?
- How often do you spend a lot of time on obsessive thoughts or compulsions?
- How often do you try to ignore or suppress your obsessions?
- How often do you feel distressed by your obsessions or compulsions?
- How often do you avoid situations that trigger your obsessions?
- How often do you seek reassurance from others?
- How often do you feel like your rituals interfere with your daily life?
