# Bipolar Disorder Questions — Open-Ended Question Bank

**PENDING CLINICAL SCOPE REVIEW** (see ANAHAT_Knowledge_Architecture_Report.md, Migration Plan item 3): these psychiatric-condition-specific questions may sit outside ANAHAT's stated non-diagnostic scope and the Governance Protocol's rule against definitive clinical conclusions. Do not wire this folder into the Question Selection Agent's retrieval scope until a clinical lead confirms inclusion.

- How often do you experience periods of elevated mood or mania?
- How often do you feel overly energetic or restless during these periods?
- How often do you have racing thoughts or talk rapidly?
- How often do you engage in risky behaviors during manic episodes?
- How often do you experience depressive episodes?
- How often do you have trouble sleeping during manic periods?
- How often do you feel grandiose or overly confident?
- How often do you have suicidal thoughts during depressive episodes?
- How often do you experience mixed episodes (both manic and depressive symptoms)?
- How often do you have difficulty maintaining relationships due to mood swings?
