# Past — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Are you uncomfortable discussing anything from past events?
- What would you say about your early years?
- What are a few of your favorite childhood memories?
- What characteristics and beliefs have been influenced by your past?
- Is there a particular moment in your life you would like to share about how your values or viewpoint changed significantly?
- Do you still feel the effects of any major past events on you now?
- Would you like to go back and revisit any past experiences?
- Do you have a special family ritual or tradition from your early years that you cherish?
- Do you see any recurring themes or patterns in your earlier experiences?
- If so, how do you deal with them in your present life?
- How does nostalgia affect your current feelings and what part does it play in your thoughts about the past?
- Do you feel that unresolved problems from previous experiences are impeding your happiness or personal development?
- What aspects of your past decisions—both successful and unsuccessful—still affect the way you make decisions now?
- What effects did the good and bad relationships you had in the past have on your capacity to trust people?
