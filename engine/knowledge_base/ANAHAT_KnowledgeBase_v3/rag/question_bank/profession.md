# Profession — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Could you tell me a bit about the nature of your work or studies -- is it more managerial, technical, creative, or supportive in nature?
- What does your day-to-day work usually involve -- leading a team, meeting targets, client interaction?
- How would you describe your work environment -- high-pressure, collaborative, structured, or flexible?
- What's been the toughest part of work lately -- workload, deadlines, or managing a team?
- When work gets stressful, what do you usually do to cope?
- How do you feel about where you are professionally -- ambitious for more, content, or looking for a change?
- How do you usually feel about taking on new projects at work -- eager, hesitant, or open depending on the situation?
- Questions About Your Lifestyle How would you describe your daily routine -- fairly active, mostly sedentary, or a good balance of both?
- How would you rate your work-life balance right now -- well-maintained, a struggle, or overworked?
- How has your sleep been lately -- sound and restful, disturbed, or are you dealing with insomnia?
- What do you usually turn to when you need to manage stress -- yoga, mindfulness, spending time with people, or do you tend to withdraw?
- How do you like to unwind -- hobbies, just relaxing, watching TV, or getting outdoors?
- How would you describe your self-care routine these days -- regular, occasional, or something you feel needs attention?
- How involved are you in household responsibilities -- actively, minimally, or do you mostly delegate?
