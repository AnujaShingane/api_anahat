# Personal Interests — Open-Ended Question Bank

- What do you enjoy doing in your free time -- reading, music, dance, gardening, art?
- When you're learning something new, do you prefer to see it, hear it, try it hands-on, or read about it?
- Do you have any creative outlets you turn to -- painting, writing, singing?
- When you travel, are you more adventurous, or do you prefer comfort and familiar experiences?
- Are you currently working on learning something new -- through courses, workshops, or on your own?
- Do you enjoy reading?
- If so, what kind -- fiction, non-fiction, self-help, biographies?
- Do you have any spiritual or religious practices, like meditation or prayer, that are part of your life?
