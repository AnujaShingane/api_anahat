# Thoughts — Open-Ended Question Bank

- What would you say best describes the primary thoughts that cross your head every day?
- Would you like to look into any negative thought patterns that seem to come back?
- What is your approach to dealing with self-critical thoughts, and how do they affect your sense of worth as an individual?
- Is there anything that you can see that might be causing you to perceive reality differently?
- How much do your current cognitive processes originate from your previous experiences?
- Are you prone to having intrusive or nervous thoughts in certain situations?
- What is your procedure for making decisions, and are there any particular ideas that get in the way?
- Do you have any instinctive beliefs that exacerbate stress or feelings of being overwhelmed?
- What effects do your thoughts have on your capacity to establish and accomplish goals?
- In what ways do you strike a balance in your everyday thinking between acceptable and inappropriate ideas?
- Do you see any patterns of thinking that might be causing problems in your relationships?
- What effects does your self-talk have on your determination and ability to persevere in the face of difficulty?
- Are you always thinking about any unsolved problems or unanswered questions?
- How do you deal with overthinking, and does it make it harder for you to unwind?
- Which techniques do you regularly use to encourage constructive and optimistic thinking?
