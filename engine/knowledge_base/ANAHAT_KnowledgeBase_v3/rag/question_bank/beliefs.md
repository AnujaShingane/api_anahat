# Beliefs — Open-Ended Question Bank

- What effects do your underlying beliefs have on how you see the world and yourself?
- Is there any belief that you can think of that limits your growth or well-being?
- How have your relationships beliefs changed over time, and how do they affect how you connect with other people?
- What fundamental beliefs do you have about success and failure, and how do they influence the way you tackle obstacles?
- What aspects of your identity and decision-making are influenced by your cultural or religious beliefs?
- Did you ever doubt or reevaluate some of your long-held beliefs?
- If so, why did you think that way?
- To what extent does your mental and emotional health depend on what you believe about your own value and affection?
- Which belief systems in your life can be a cause behind your tension or anxiety?
- How can you better handle life transitions if your beliefs about change and adaptability are different?
- Do any messages or beliefs from your early years still affect the way you think and act now?
- How does your sense of purpose and involvement in the community stem from your political or social beliefs?
- Which decisions and priorities in life are influenced by your beliefs about happiness and fulfillment?
- Have you seen any changes in your beliefs as a result of remarkable experiences or personal development?
- What are your methods for finding balance and how do you resolve contradictory beliefs inside yourself?
- Could you describe a situation in which refuting or altering a certain belief resulted in a beneficial change in yourself?
