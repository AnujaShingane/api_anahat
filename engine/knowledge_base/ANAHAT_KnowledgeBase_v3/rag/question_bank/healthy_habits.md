# Healthy Habits — Open-Ended Question Bank

- What benefits do your present healthful activities provide for your general well-being?
- Do you have any particular daily practices that promote your mental and physical well-being?
- How well do your food choices support your overall wellness and health objectives?
- Do you have any bad habits that you think might be affecting your general health?
- As sleep is essential to living a healthy lifestyle, how do you prioritize getting enough of it?
- How do you stay motivated to be active?
- What part does regular exercise represent in your daily routine?
- What particular methods or strategies do you employ to handle stress in every aspect of your life?
- How do you keep a balanced fluid intake throughout the day and make sure you’re properly hydrated?
- To enhance your mental well-being, do you engage in mindfulness or meditation practices?
- If so, how has it changed your day-to-day activities?
- What actions do you take to keep a good work-life balance, and how has it affected your general well-being?
- In everyday routine, how do you maintain a healthy relationship with technology and regulate screen time?
- How do you respond to obstacles or failures in upholding your healthful patterns?
- Do you adhere to any particular dietary guidelines or nutritional targets in order to maintain a healthy lifestyle?
- How can your general health and well-being benefit from your relationships and social connections?
