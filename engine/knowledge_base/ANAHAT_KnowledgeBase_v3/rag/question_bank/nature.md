# Nature — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Would you describe yourself as more of an introvert, an extrovert, or a bit of both?
- When something's bothering you, do you tend to speak up directly, or do you hold back and go along with things?
- Would you say you generally look at life with optimism, or do you tend to see things more realistically or idealistically?
- Are you someone who likes to take risks, or do you prefer to stay within your comfort zone?
- How would you describe your relationship with time -- do you plan ahead, or do things tend to pile up till the last minute?
- Do you find it easy to forgive yourself and others, or do certain things tend to stay with you?
- Would you say you have a sharp memory, or do day-to-day things tend to slip your mind?
- In a group setting, do you naturally take the lead, prefer to follow, or find it hard to hand things off to others?
- When you're under stress, what usually happens -- do you overthink, get frustrated, or manage to stay calm?
