# Social Circle — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Would you say you have a large circle of friends, or do you keep your circle small and selective?
- Who do you usually turn to when you need to talk -- family, friends, or colleagues?
- How reliable would you say your support system is when you need someone?
- How active are you on social media -- would you say often, occasionally, or barely at all?
- What do you usually enjoy doing with friends -- sports, movies, travel, or maybe spiritual gatherings?
- What would your friends say you're known for?
- What matters most to you in a friendship -- trust, fun, emotional support, or shared interests?
