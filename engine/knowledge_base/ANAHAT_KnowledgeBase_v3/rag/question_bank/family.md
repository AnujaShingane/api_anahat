# Family — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Do you come from a nuclear family or a joint family setup?
- Who would you say makes up your immediate family -- parents, siblings, spouse, children?
- How would you describe your bond with your family -- strong, just okay, or something you're still working through?
- What role do you usually find yourself playing in your family -- caregiver, supportive, or maybe more of a breadwinner?
- Growing up and even now, would you say the atmosphere at home feels more positive and supportive, or stressful?
- Would you describe your family as close-knit, a bit distant, or somewhere with ongoing conflicts?
- What does family mean to you personally -- a source of support, a responsibility, or something else?
