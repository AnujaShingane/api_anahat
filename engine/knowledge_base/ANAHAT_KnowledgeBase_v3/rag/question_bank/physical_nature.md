# Physical Nature — Open-Ended Question Bank

- Do you follow any kind of fitness routine -- walking, running, yoga, gym?
- How often would you say you exercise -- daily, weekly, or fairly inconsistently?
- How would you describe how you feel about your body right now -- positive, neutral, or a bit self-conscious?
- Are there any current health concerns you're dealing with -- weight, digestion, anxiety, or something else?
- Is there any family history of health conditions like diabetes, hypertension, or heart issues?
- If you experience physical pain, what do you usually turn to for relief -- music, medication, physical therapy?
- Would you say you keep up with regular check-ups and preventive care, or does that tend to get put off?
