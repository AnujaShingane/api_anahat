# Integrated ANAHAT Assessment Design v3

These files are part of the ANAHAT Knowledge Base. They define the proposed end-to-end assessment protocol.

Key decisions:
1. Current issue is the strongest initial signal for quadrant relevance.
2. Demographics personalize communication and context; they do not directly score chakra imbalance or quadrant relevance.
3. Fixed opening questions are therapist-controlled.
4. After the therapist selects a quadrant, the AI selects patient-specific questions from approved KB material and avoids generic repetition.
5. The therapist records the patient's response; the engine extracts structured evidence.
6. Multi-chakra indicators must be clarified before they are treated as confirmed evidence.
7. Two chakra findings are reported only after an explicit evidence gate; ranking first is not enough.
8. The therapist can continue, override, or stop the assessment at any point.
9. Raga/prahar mapping occurs only after therapist-approved top-two chakra findings.
10. The prescription is editable and the patient receives only the therapist-approved final version.

Clinical lead review is required before production use. Chakra associations are the project's complementary framework and should not be represented as medical causation or diagnosis.
